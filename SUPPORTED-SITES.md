# Supported sites

Libregaku draws interactive subtitles over video on these sites. It uses the subtitles the site
itself offers, in the language you are learning. A video with no subtitles in that language has
nothing to show, and Libregaku says so. It does not machine-translate missing subtitles.

| Site | Where | Sentence audio on a card | Still image on a card |
|---|---|---|---|
| YouTube | www.youtube.com | From the video | Yes |
| Netflix | www.netflix.com | Recorded from the tab | No |
| Disney+ | www.disneyplus.com | Recorded from the tab | No |
| Prime Video | www.primevideo.com | Recorded from the tab | No |
| HBO Max | play.hbomax.com, www.hbomax.com | Recorded from the tab | No |
| Apple TV | tv.apple.com | Recorded from the tab | No |
| Viki | www.viki.com | Recorded from the tab | No |

You watch as you always do, signed in to your own account. Libregaku needs no account of its own.

## Protected sites

Every site above but YouTube encrypts its video. Libregaku cannot take sound or a picture from an
encrypted video, so it records the tab instead, which Chrome allows only once you have said so:

1. Open Settings → Anki cards → Sentence audio on protected sites, and press Allow.
2. On the tab the video plays in, click the Libregaku icon in the toolbar and press Record
   sentence audio here. Do this once per tab.

Subtitles, lookups and cards without sound work on every site with no permission at all.

## When a site changes

These sites change their pages without warning, and when one does, subtitles can stop appearing.
Libregaku checks every site each day for the changes it would notice, and when one breaks, the
extension says which part stopped working rather than showing nothing.

If subtitles stop appearing on a site in this list, please
[open an issue](https://github.com/donth77/libregaku/issues) with the site and the address of the
video.
