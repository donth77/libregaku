Get started

Two choices, and everything else has a sensible default.

  1. 1 Choose what you're learning
  2. 2 Connect Anki and choose a deck for new cards

Anki is optional — subtitles, lookups and furigana all work without it. It is what remembers which words you already know.

Then open a video with subtitles in the language you are learning, and hover a word. The keyboard button in the transcript panel lists the keyboard shortcuts.
